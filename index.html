Windows in place. Instantly.

Raster snaps the active window to halves, quarters and thirds with a single shortcut – or tiles every window on your display at once. A quiet menu bar app designed for macOS Tahoe.

Download on the Mac App Store Free on GitHub

Mac App Store: coming soon, currently in review · macOS 26 or later · Apple silicon & Intel · English & German · Free & open source

◧

Halves & maximize

⌘← and ⌘→ snap the window to the left or right half, ⌘↑ maximizes it. Press again to restore.

⇆

Press again to resize

Repeat a half shortcut and the width cycles ½ → ⅔ → ⅓ – or, if you prefer, the window jumps to the neighboring display.

▦

Quarters & thirds

Quarters, thirds, two-thirds, almost maximized, centered and restore – each with its own shortcut.

⊞

All windows at once

Tile every window on a display as a grid, side by side in columns, or as focus + stack: the active window left, the rest stacked on the right.

⧉

Multiple displays

Raster uses the display under the mouse pointer – or the active window's display, if you prefer. Move windows to the next display with a shortcut.

⌘

Your shortcuts, your gap

Change every shortcut with one click and set the gap between windows from 0 to 24 points.

Every shortcut can be changed in Settings.

One panel in the menu bar

Click the Raster item to open a Liquid Glass panel with every layout as a tile. Light and dark mode automatically.

Stays out of your way

Need ⌘← in a text field? Pause the shortcuts from the right-click menu, or assign different keys.

Private by design

No data collection, no account, no ads, no subscription. Raster never opens a network connection – not even once.
